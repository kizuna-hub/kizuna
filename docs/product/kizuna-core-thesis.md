# Kizuna Core Product Thesis

## What Kizuna is

Kizuna Hub is a decision-and-evidence operating layer for early-stage ventures.

It helps a founder turn scattered venture context and trusted feedback into a traceable sequence:

```text
clear decision
  -> testable action
    -> evidence
      -> recorded outcome
        -> explainable progress
```

The founder’s primary job is to understand the most important unresolved decision, why it matters now, and what action will produce useful evidence. Kizuna should make that job obvious before it exposes supporting tools.

## What Kizuna is not

Kizuna is not primarily:

- a mentor marketplace;
- a startup course or incubator replacement;
- a pitch-deck generator;
- a readiness-score dashboard;
- a product-discovery community;
- an investor data-room platform.

Those capabilities may support the operating model, but none of them is the product center.

## Projects and ventures

The user-facing term is **Project** because it is familiar to founders. The canonical domain term is **Venture** because it describes the durable business identity across decisions, cycles, evidence, programs, and support relationships.

One Project in the interface maps to one Venture in the domain.

## Human support is conditional, not assumed

A venture may have:

- an existing primary mentor;
- a mentor assigned by a program or competition;
- one or more informal advisors;
- a specialist or guest reviewer;
- no active support relationship.

Mentor discovery is conditional support for a known gap. It is not the default next step and is never shown as a generic feed merely because no mentor exists.

External mentors, competitions, incubators, and programs remain first-class context. Kizuna coordinates decisions and evidence around those relationships; it does not require that the relationship originate inside Kizuna.

## Phase 1 scope

Phase 1 establishes Product Foundation and Venture Entry:

- canonical venture terminology and type-safe domain;
- deterministic multi-venture demo state;
- state-aware founder entry;
- global and venture-scoped navigation;
- Projects portfolio and project switching;
- Venture Overview centered on phase, blocked decision, rationale, next action, and support status;
- minimum intake handoff into the canonical state;
- compatibility for useful AI Pitch and Data Room work.

Readiness may appear only as secondary context. It cannot replace the decision narrative.

## Later-phase boundaries

Phase 1 does not implement:

- context import and source provenance review;
- assumption challenge or decision selection workflows;
- action-cycle creation, experiment management, or task management;
- mentor brief generation, feedback capture, or feedback reconciliation;
- evidence submission/review, outcome review, or explainable readiness deltas;
- Program Mode, opportunity matching, specialist matching, or mentor discovery;
- production persistence, authentication, file delivery, calendar, email, or AI services.

The Phase 1 domain includes extension points for these flows so later phases can add behavior without replacing venture identity or state ownership.

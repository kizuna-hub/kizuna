# Narrative and Voice

Kizuna language is calm, specific, credible, and human. The workspace helps people make decisions and complete work; it does not sound like a generic AI product or a marketing funnel.

## Shared thesis

> Build the proof before you ask for belief.

In application UI, translate the thesis into concrete next actions rather than repeating the tagline on every screen.

## Role promises

| Role | Kizuna helps them | Desired feeling |
|---|---|---|
| Founder | Organize context, evidence, milestones, and the current ask. | "I know what to improve before review." |
| Mentor | Review relevant context and give useful, scoped feedback. | "I can contribute judgment instead of generic advice." |
| Investor | Discover and evaluate opportunities with evidence behind the pitch. | "I can understand readiness and risk efficiently." |

## Preferred terminology

| Prefer | Avoid unless literally accurate |
|---|---|
| proof, evidence, evidence trail | hype, guaranteed result |
| current ask | pitch request |
| mentor-ready | investor-approved |
| pitch readiness | funding score |
| context behind the pitch | secure/full data room when not implemented |
| meaningful introduction | perfect match |
| relevant discovery | qualified lead when not verified |
| workspace | dashboard when referring to the broader product |

## Product copy principles

1. **Actionable:** Buttons state the result: "Run readiness review", "Generate share link", "Request mentor feedback".
2. **Honest:** Demo/local/mock behavior is labeled; AI is advisory rather than a final decision maker.
3. **Role-aware:** Permission, access, and next-step copy reflects the current role.
4. **Recoverable:** Error and empty states explain what happened and what to do next.
5. **Calm:** Avoid urgency theater, exclamation-heavy headings, and unsupported superlatives.

## Claims to avoid

- Guaranteed funding, matching, acceptance, or outcomes.
- "Investor approved", "AI gatekeeper", or autonomous final decisions.
- "Bank-level security", "zero leaks", or immutable records without implementation evidence.
- Vetted/verified claims without a real process.
- Fake testimonials, fabricated metrics, or fake activity presented as live data.

Public landing section order, SEO copy, and conversion language remain landing-only context. They are not workspace information architecture.

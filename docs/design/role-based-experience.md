# Role-Based Experience

Founder, Mentor, and Investor use one visual system and primitive layer. Their information architecture, permissions, decision cadence, and density may differ.

## Shared product model

```text
Founder builds context and evidence
  -> Mentor contributes judgment and feedback
    -> Investor discovers and evaluates relevant opportunities
```

The model describes information flow, not automatic matching, approval, or funding.

## Founder

Primary question: **What should I improve before review?**

- Prioritize current project, readiness, missing evidence, milestones, and the next useful action.
- Use guidance and explanation where a student founder may not know the expected artifact.
- Keep AI advisory and expose the evidence behind readiness suggestions.
- Preserve the existing Founder feature-first modules and demo-state flow.

## Mentor

Primary question: **Where can my judgment be most useful?**

- Prioritize requests, relevant context, review scope, scheduling, feedback state, and reputation evidence.
- Make founder evidence scannable before asking for feedback.
- Distinguish pending, accepted, completed, and unavailable actions.
- Do not copy Founder onboarding or Investor pipeline IA.

## Investor

Primary question: **Which opportunities deserve deeper evaluation and why?**

- Prioritize sourcing, filters, saved views, deal stages, evidence/risk, portfolio context, and collaboration.
- Support denser tables and comparison while retaining accessible hierarchy.
- Permission and subscription gates are explicit and non-deceptive.
- AI sourcing/radar language remains advisory and explainable.

## Permission rules

- Hide actions that are irrelevant to a role when discovery provides no value.
- Show read-only state when visibility matters but mutation is not allowed.
- Explain meaningful gates and preserve a real next step.
- Never fork a primitive to encode role permissions; permission logic belongs in features.

## Visual consistency test

If role labels and data are removed, buttons, inputs, focus rings, panels, statuses, overlays, and typography should still look and behave like one Kizuna product.
